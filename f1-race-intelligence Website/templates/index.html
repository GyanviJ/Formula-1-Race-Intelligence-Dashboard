01 — THE PROJECT

Beyond the Finish Line.

Turning Formula 1 race data into meaningful performance insights.

Formula 1 is more than speed. It is a combination of driver skill, team performance, circuit characteristics, reliability, and strategy.

This project uses Tableau visualisations to explore historical race data, identify performance patterns, compare competitors, and present racing information through an interactive analytical experience.
